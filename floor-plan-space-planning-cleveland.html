Measure Twice, Move Once

The most overlooked step in any senior move.

Downsizing into a smaller home means not everything fits — and finding that out on moving day, with a sofa wedged in a stairwell, is how moves go sideways.

We prevent it. We get the new home's floor plan, measure every must-keep piece of furniture, and map it to scale — confirming what fits, what doesn't, and where it all goes before a single box is packed.

The result is a move with no wasted trips, no furniture that has to turn around at the door, and a new home that's arranged thoughtfully instead of wherever the movers set it down.

What this includes

  • New-home floor plan obtained
  • Furniture measured to the inch
  • To-scale layout drawings
  • Doorway, hallway & elevator checks
  • Keep / sell decisions informed by fit
  • A clear move-day placement plan

How It Works
