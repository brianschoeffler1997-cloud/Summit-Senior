Honest Questions

Quick answers, before you read.

✦Is this really free? What's the catch?
There isn't one. We wrote the guide because we wanted the information to exist for Cleveland families whether they hire us or not. Read it, share it with siblings, forward it to a neighbor going through the same thing. No catch, no email gate, no follow-up calls.
✦I'm not in Cleveland. Is this still useful?
Yes — about 90% of the advice applies anywhere. The references to specific Cleveland senior communities (Menorah Park, Judson, South Franklin Circle) won't be relevant outside Northeast Ohio, but the framework, the order of operations, and the practical advice are universal.
✦Can I print it out and use it as a workbook?
Yes — that's exactly how we hoped it would get used. Open the PDF, click your browser's print button, save as PDF or print to paper. Every step page has a 5-item checklist with real checkboxes you can tick off as you go.
✦What if I want to talk to someone about my parent's move?
Call us directly at 216-236-3966. Brian answers the phone himself, and the first conversation is free and pressure-free. We're not going to chase you — you reach out when (and if) you're ready.
