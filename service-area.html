Local, Not Regional

A real local business, not a national franchise.

Most senior-moving companies in Cleveland are franchises of a national brand. We're not. Summit is owner-operated and based right here in Greater Cleveland — which means the person who shows up to your consultation is the same person who'll be in your parent's house on move day.

Our service area covers the six Northeast Ohio counties below. If you don't see your city in the lists, please reach out anyway — chances are we can help, especially for communities just outside these lines.
