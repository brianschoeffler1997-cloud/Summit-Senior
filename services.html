Full Service Menu

Eight ways we help — or just the parts you need.

Every plan is custom. We'll never sell you services you don't need, and we won't withhold ones you do. Below is everything we handle, in roughly the order most families use them.

01

Downsizing & Sorting

Thoughtful, room-by-room decisions about what to keep, donate, sell, or discard — at your pace and with zero pressure. We do the heavy lifting, the boxing, and the load-out so the family never has to.

Room-by-room sorting Heirloom round Decision documentation Load-out
02

Packing & Unpacking

Professional, careful packing of an entire home or just specific rooms — and full unpacking and setup at the new location so it feels like home the moment you walk in.

Full or partial Fragile-grade materials Setup at new home
03

Senior Move Coordination

We personally handle the move — sorting, packing, loading, transport, unloading, unpacking, and setup at the new home. No third-party moving company, no rotating crew. The same hands that box it up at your parent's house unbox it at theirs.

We do the moving On-site supervision Damage prevention Same-day setup
04

Online Item Sales

A private, modern alternative to the traditional estate sale. We photograph, list, and sell items on Facebook Marketplace and other platforms — handling all buyer inquiries, pickups, and transactions so the family never has to.

Professional photos Listing & pricing Buyer coordination Pickup management
05

Donation & Disposal

We coordinate donations to local Cleveland charities, arrange responsible junk removal, and make sure as little as possible ends up in a landfill. Nothing is left for the family to deal with afterward.

Local charities Junk hauling Tax receipts
06

Home Cleanout

After a sale or move, we can arrange a full cleanout of the property — leaving it clean, empty, and ready for listing or handover. Especially helpful when the family is selling and lives out of state.

Full property cleanout Listing-ready Out-of-state support
07

Settling-In Support

The part most movers skip. We hang pictures, connect electronics, organize closets, set up the kitchen, and stay until your parent or loved one can find their morning coffee without thinking about it.

Picture hanging TV & tech setup Closet organization Kitchen setup
08

Floor Plan & Space Planning

We get the new home's floor plan, measure every piece of must-keep furniture, and decide what fits before moving day — not after the sectional can't make the doorway.

To-scale layouts Furniture measure-up Doorway & elevator check

Who We Serve

Built for seniors and the families helping them.

For Seniors

A transition handled with respect.

You've earned a move that's done with patience and dignity — not pressure to go faster than you're comfortable with. We protect what matters most and make sure your next home feels like home from day one.

For Adult Children

Boots on the ground in Cleveland.

Managing a parent's move from across town — or across the country — is overwhelming. We become your local hands and eyes, with clear, proactive updates by phone, text, or email at every step.

Family-Home Downsizing

From a full house to a smaller space.

Decades of belongings, no clear place to start. We guide you through sorting, donating, selling, and letting go — without the stress, and without pressure to part with anything you're not ready to.

Senior-Living Moves

Independent, assisted, or memory care.

Moves into a care community require precision. We coordinate directly with the community's move-in team so the right items arrive in the right rooms at the right time, with minimal disruption.

How It Works

Simple from the very first call.

02
