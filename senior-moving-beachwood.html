We Know Beachwood Homes — and the Communities Families Move To

Most Beachwood families we help are downsizing from a four- or five-bedroom colonial — homes built between the 1960s and 1980s along streets like South Woodland, Shaker Boulevard, and Cedar Road. Decades of belongings in a basement and three closets. A move to senior living that's measured in weeks, not months.


What sets a Beachwood move apart isn't the boxes — it's the coordination. The receiving community often has strict move-in windows. The estate may be in trust. Adult children are usually managing it from Columbus, Chicago, or further. We handle all of that in one phone number.
