From spacious homes to the right-sized space.

Solon is full of large, comfortable family homes — and when it's time to right-size, there's a lot to thoughtfully work through. We help families decide what fits the next home and handle the move end to end.

We regularly coordinate move-ins to Solon's senior-living communities, including The Weils and Vitalia of Solon, working directly with their teams on timing and logistics. The same hands that pack the old home set up the new one.

Local means easy scheduling and no travel surcharge — just a straightforward consultation at your Solon home whenever it suits you.
