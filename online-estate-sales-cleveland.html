Beyond the Traditional Estate Sale

Get fair value for the furniture and keepsakes — without opening the home to crowds.

A traditional estate sale means strangers wandering through a lifetime's home, haggling over the dining table in the dining room where it's always stood. For many families, that feels wrong — and it often leaves the most valuable pieces underpriced.

We offer a quieter, more dignified alternative. We photograph items well, research fair prices, and list them online where the right buyers are actually looking. Furniture, antiques, china, tools, collectibles, and décor reach a far wider audience than a single weekend sale ever could.

Then we handle the part nobody wants: fielding messages, scheduling pickups, meeting buyers, and managing payment. The family gets a clear accounting and the proceeds — without ever fielding a 9 p.m. text from a stranger about the curio cabinet.

What this includes

  • Professional item photography
  • Price research & fair listing
  • Listings on Marketplace & more
  • All buyer inquiries handled
  • Pickup scheduling & meetups
  • Clear proceeds accounting
