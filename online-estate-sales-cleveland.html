The Estate Sale, Reinvented

All the value, none of the open house.

A traditional estate sale means opening the family home to the public for a weekend — strangers in the bedrooms, haggling at the kitchen table, and whatever doesn't sell still sitting there Monday morning.

We do it online instead. We photograph the items worth selling, research fair prices, and list them on Facebook Marketplace. We field every message, vet every buyer, and coordinate every pickup — the family never deals with a stranger.

It's more private, more flexible, and often faster than a one-weekend sale. And it pairs naturally with downsizing: the things that don't make the move can become the budget that funds it.

How the Agreement Works

Clear terms, agreed up front — no surprises later.

We focus on items worth at least $100 and sell them on Facebook Marketplace. Before anything is listed, we build a log of every item and agree with you on a list price for each one. Here's exactly how the rest works.

01

$100 Minimum

We list items we believe are worth at least $100, so the time and effort behind every listing is worth it for you.

02

Itemized Log & Agreed Pricing

Every item gets logged with an Initial List Price you sign off on before it ever goes live — so you always know what's being sold and for how much we're asking.

03

Negotiating Authority

To keep sales moving, you grant us authority to negotiate within 15% of the Initial List Price without checking back in for every offer.

04

Payout Timing

Once a buyer's funds have cleared, your remittance is sent within 7 business days — no waiting until the whole project wraps up.

05

Unsold Items After 60 Days

If an item hasn't sold after 60 days, we'll notify you. It needs to be retrieved within 7 days of that notice, or it's considered abandoned and we'll responsibly route it to donation or disposal.
