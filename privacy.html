Privacy policy.

Short version: we collect only what we need to schedule your free consultation, and we never sell or share your information.

Last updated: May 2026 · Summit Senior Services · Cleveland, Ohio

1. Who we are

Summit Senior Services is a locally-owned senior moving and downsizing company based in Greater Cleveland, Ohio, operated by Brian Schoeffler. You can reach us at info@SummitSeniorServicesOhio.com or (216) 236-3966.

2. What we collect

When you submit our consultation form or contact us by phone, email, or text, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (if provided)
  • Your city or service area
  • Your preferred timeline
  • Any details you choose to share about your situation

Our website also uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site. This produces anonymized, aggregated data — page views, browser type, time on site. It does not identify you personally.

3. How we use it

We use the information you provide for one purpose: to follow up about your free consultation and, if you become a client, to coordinate your move. Specifically, we use your contact details to:

  • Schedule your in-home consultation
  • Send you a written plan and quote after we meet
  • Keep you updated during the project, if you hire us

We do not add you to a marketing list, send you newsletters, or follow up after you've told us you're not interested.

4. What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Period. We don't advertise on social media platforms that buy contact lists, and we don't trade leads with referral services. Your information stays with us.

5. How we store it

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. Once we receive your inquiry, we store the basic contact details in standard business tools (email, calendar, and a project-management system) protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication.

6. How long we keep it

If you contact us and decide not to work with Summit, we keep your information for one year in case you reach back out — then delete it. If you become a client, we retain project records for seven years for tax, insurance, and business purposes.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you what information we have about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information from our records

Email info@SummitSeniorServicesOhio.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" and we'll respond within five business days.

8. Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies for basic functionality (e.g., keeping the navigation menu open on mobile) and for the Google Analytics tracking mentioned above. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the site.

9. Updates to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Substantive changes won't apply retroactively to information already collected.

10. Questions

If anything here is unclear, just ask. Call (216) 236-3966 or email info@SummitSeniorServicesOhio.com and you'll get a real reply from a real person — same as everything else we do.
