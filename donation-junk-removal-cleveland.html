The Last Mile of Any Move

Nothing left for the family to deal with.

After the keepers are boxed and the valuables are sold, there's always a remainder — the usable-but-unwanted, the worn-out, the genuinely done. Handling it is the part families dread, and the part we quietly take off their hands.

Usable goods go to local Cleveland-area charities that will actually use them, with donation receipts handled for your records. The rest is sorted for recycling or responsible disposal.

The goal is simple: the home ends up clear, the family ends up with nothing left to schedule, and as little as possible ends up in a landfill.

What this includes

  • Donation routing to local charities
  • Donation receipts for your records
  • Responsible junk hauling
  • Recycling wherever possible
  • Heavy lifting and load-out
  • Left clear, with nothing to schedule

Where Things Go

Re-homed first. Hauled only as a last resort.

We try to keep usable things in use. Furniture, housewares, and clothing are matched to charities that want them, not dumped because it's easier.

Only what genuinely can't be re-homed gets recycled or disposed of — and we handle the receipts so the donation counts.
