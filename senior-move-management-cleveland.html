No Handoffs

The same hands, the whole way through.

The usual senior move is a relay race: a sorter, a packer, a moving company, and a setup crew — none of whom talk to each other, all of whom bill separately. Things fall through the cracks at every handoff.

We do it differently. One team, one point of contact, one plan — from the first sort to the last picture hung. The person who wrapped your mother's china is the person who unwraps it in her new kitchen.

That continuity is the whole point. Nothing gets lost in translation, nobody says "that's not our department," and you make one phone call instead of five.

How It Works

How move day actually goes.

Four coordinated stages, all handled by the same team — most moves are done and set up in a single day.

01

Plan

We confirm the timeline, the inventory, the new floor plan, and any community move-in requirements ahead of time.

02

Pack & Load

We pack what isn't already boxed, protect the home, and load in a deliberate order for a safe, efficient trip.

03

Transport & Unload

We drive it ourselves and unload directly into the right rooms at the new home — no staging limbo.

04

Set Up & Settle

Furniture placed, beds made, kitchen working, boxes removed. We leave it livable, not a maze of cardboard.
