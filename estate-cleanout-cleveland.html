When the House Itself Has to Move On

An empty, clean home is the difference between a listing that's ready and one that waits.

Once a loved one has moved or passed, the home often has to be sold — and a realtor can't list a house that's still full of decades of belongings. For a family living out of state, flying in to empty a four-bedroom home isn't realistic. That's exactly when a full cleanout matters most.

We clear everything that's left, top to bottom: furniture, closets, the basement, the attic, the garage, the shed. Keepers are set aside for the family, valuables go to sale, usable goods to donation, and the rest is responsibly removed. The result is a genuinely empty home.

Then we leave it broom-clean and presentable — ready for photos, a showing, or the handoff of keys. We coordinate with realtors and out-of-state families by phone and photo, so the whole thing can happen without anyone having to be in the room.

  • Keep / sell / donate / dispose sorting

How It Works

How a full cleanout runs.

A complete, coordinated clear-out designed for families who can't be there in person.

01

Assess the Property

We walk the home (in person or by video) and build a plan covering every room and storage space, plus the family's keepers.

02

Sort & Route

We separate keepers, sale items, donations, and disposal, and route each to the right destination as we clear.

03

Clear & Haul

We empty the property completely, hauling everything ourselves — no leftover piles, no half-finished rooms.

04

Clean & Confirm

We leave the home broom-clean and send photos confirming it's empty and listing-ready, coordinating with your agent if needed.
