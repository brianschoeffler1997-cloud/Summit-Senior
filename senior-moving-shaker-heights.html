Local to Shaker Heights

We Know Shaker Homes — and the Communities Families Move To

Most Shaker Heights families we help are downsizing from a historic Tudor revival, English cottage, or center-hall Colonial — homes built between the 1910s and 1940s along streets like Fairmount Boulevard, Shaker Boulevard, South Park, and North Park. Five bedrooms, a third-floor servant's wing, an attic the family hasn't touched in thirty years, and a basement full of estate furniture worth real money.


What makes a Shaker move different is the volume and the value. There's almost always furniture worth selling rather than donating, art that should be appraised before anything moves, and a deep emotional weight to leaving a home a family has owned for two or three generations. We move thoughtfully and slowly. No one is rushing your parent out of Shaker Heights.
