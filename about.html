Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Brian Schoeffler.

I started Summit Senior Services because I know firsthand how overwhelming this process can be. Whether a family is helping a parent downsize after decades in the same home, or navigating a move to assisted living, the emotional weight alone can feel like too much — before you've even thought about the logistics.

My passion is simple: I want to be the person families in Greater Cleveland can call and genuinely feel relieved afterward. Not more stressed. Not more confused. Relieved. That's the standard I hold myself to on every single job.

I believe the transition to a new chapter of life deserves patience, dignity, and a personal touch. You shouldn't have to coordinate five different vendors, chase down movers, or figure out what to do with a houseful of belongings on your own. That's exactly what I'm here for.

When you work with Summit Senior Services, you're not hiring a faceless company. You're working directly with me — someone who genuinely cares about your family's outcome and won't consider the job done until you're truly settled and at ease.

How I Work

A few rules I've set for every job.

The same principles, whether it's a 5-bedroom downsizing or a single closet sort.

01

The first conversation is free, and it's not a pitch.

The in-home consult is a real conversation about your situation — what you need, what your parent needs, what the timeline looks like. If we're not the right fit, I'll tell you. If you want to think about it for six months, that's fine too. You'll never get a follow-up sales call from me.

02

You see the plan and the price before anything starts.

Every job gets a written plan: what we're doing, when, and what it costs. No hidden fees, no surprise line items, no "we hit an obstacle, here's another invoice." If the scope changes, we talk about it first.

03

Your pace, always.

The biggest mistake in senior moves is rushing them. Decades of belongings don't get sorted in a weekend, and parents shouldn't feel hurried out of their own decisions. I'll work with whatever timeline you have — even if that means three months of one slow afternoon a week.

04

One point of contact, start to finish.

You don't get handed off to a foreman, an estimator, or a billing department. You call me, you text me, you email me — and I'm the one who shows up. That's the whole point of working with a small, owner-led business instead of a national franchise.

05

I don't disappear after move-in.

Settling-in is the part most movers skip — and it's the part that makes the new place feel like home. I stay until pictures are on the walls, electronics work, closets are organized, and your parent can find their coffee in the morning.
